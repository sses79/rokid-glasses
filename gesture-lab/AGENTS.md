# Gesture Lab

A hand-gesture playground for Rokid Glasses. The camera watches the wearer's
hand and turns its position and pose into UI input, entirely on the device.

## Capabilities

- Tracks one hand in the camera view and shows a cursor that follows it.
- Recognises an open hand, a closed fist, and left/right swipes.
- Lets the wearer toggle tiles, drag a dial, and inspect what the tracker sees.

## Boundaries

- Uses the camera only while the page is open; no frames are stored or sent.
- Does not use the network.
